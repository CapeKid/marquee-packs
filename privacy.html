Marquee privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026.

Marquee is a word puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't collect or sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, the seasons you own and a play log (the moves you make in each puzzle, used to find bugs and tune puzzles) are saved on your device. If your phone's backup is on, Android also copies them to your own Google account's backup, which only you and Google can reach, and puts them back when you reinstall. Marquee never receives that backup. A season you bought comes back from Google Play when you reinstall either way.

What leaves your device, and only when you choose

  • Sharing the play log. If you tap the button to share your play log, Android's share sheet opens and the log goes wherever you send it. Marquee never sends it on its own.
  • Sharing a result. "Share result" copies a short summary of your score to the clipboard. Nothing is sent.

Purchases

Season passes are bought through Google Play, which handles payment under Google's privacy policy. Marquee never sees your payment details. It asks Google Play which seasons your account owns and keeps only that list on your device. The play log notes when a purchase starts, finishes or fails, with the season and Google Play's result code, never order numbers or payment details.

Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, Marquee uses it for achievements and to keep a copy of your saved progress in your Play Games account, so it follows you to another phone. Sign-in is optional and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The saved copy holds the same progress and settings as the save on your device, never the play log, and only you and Google can reach it. Marquee doesn't read your name, email or contacts.

Puzzle downloads

To get new puzzles, the game downloads puzzle files from capekid.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. These requests contain no information about you or your play. Like any website, GitHub may log the IP address that made the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Marquee doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions can go to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
