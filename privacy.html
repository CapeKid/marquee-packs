Marquee privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

Marquee is a word puzzle game. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't collect or sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and a play log (the moves you make in each puzzle, used to find bugs and tune puzzles) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What leaves your device, and only when you choose

  • Sharing the play log. If you tap the button to share your play log, Android's share sheet opens and the log goes wherever you send it. Marquee never sends it on its own.
  • Sharing a result. "Share result" copies a short summary of your score to the clipboard. Nothing is sent.

Puzzle downloads

To get new puzzles, the game downloads puzzle files from capekid.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. These requests contain no information about you or your play. Like any website, GitHub may log the IP address that made the request; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Marquee doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions can go to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
